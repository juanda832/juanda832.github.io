Good questions.

Is Forma a real software product?

No. Forma is a fictional product concept created by Juanda to demonstrate website design and front-end development. All product details, people, and prices are illustrative.

What works in this preview?

The navigation, product tabs, task checkboxes, billing calculator, and demo request preview run locally in your browser.

Will my information be sent anywhere?

No. The demo request form displays a local confirmation. It does not send data, create accounts, accept payments, or store personal information.
